## Writing Process

### 1. Planning (5 minutes)
- Understand the task
- Brainstorm ideas
- Organize your thoughts
- Make a simple outline

### 2. Writing (15-20 minutes)
- Write your first draft
- Don't worry about perfection
- Follow your outline
- Keep your reader in mind

### 3. Reviewing (5 minutes)
- Check content: Does it answer the question?
- Check organization: Is it logical and clear?
- Check language: Are there grammar mistakes?
- Check spelling and punctuation

## Writing Tips

### Content
- ✓ Stay on topic
- ✓ Use specific examples
- ✓ Develop your ideas fully
- ✓ Use appropriate vocabulary

### Organization
- ✓ Have a clear beginning, middle, and end
- ✓ Use paragraphs (one main idea per paragraph)
- ✓ Use linking words (however, therefore, because, etc.)
- ✓ Make your writing flow smoothly

### Language
- ✓ Use a variety of sentence structures
- ✓ Check verb tenses
- ✓ Use appropriate register (formal/informal)
- ✓ Avoid repetition

## Common Linking Words

### Adding information
- and, also, moreover, furthermore, in addition, besides

### Contrasting
- but, however, although, though, on the other hand, in contrast

### Giving reasons
- because, since, as, due to, owing to

### Giving results
- so, therefore, thus, consequently, as a result

### Giving examples
- for example, for instance, such as, like

### Sequencing
- first, second, then, next, after that, finally

### Concluding
- in conclusion, to sum up, overall, finally

## Assessment Criteria

Good B1 writing should:
- Communicate ideas clearly
- Use simple and some complex sentences
- Have generally good grammar (some errors are okay)
- Use appropriate vocabulary
- Be organized logically
- Be 100-150 words (depending on task)
