# Reading Comprehension - B1 Level

Improve your reading skills with these texts and exercises.

## Reading Strategies

### Before Reading
- **Preview**: Look at titles, headings, and images
- **Predict**: Guess what the text will be about
- **Set a purpose**: Why are you reading this text?

### While Reading
- **Skim**: Read quickly for general understanding
- **Scan**: Look for specific information
- **Take notes**: Write down key points
- **Guess unknown words**: Use context clues
- **Visualize**: Create mental images

### After Reading
- **Summarize**: What were the main ideas?
- **Reflect**: What did you learn?
- **Review**: Look back at difficult parts

## Tips for Better Reading

- Read for 15-20 minutes every day
- Choose topics that interest you
- Don't use a dictionary for every unknown word
- Focus on understanding main ideas first
- Re-read difficult passages
- Discuss what you read with others
