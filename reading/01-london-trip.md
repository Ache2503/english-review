# A Day in London

## Reading Text

Last summer, I visited London for the first time, and it was an unforgettable experience. I had always dreamed of seeing the famous landmarks, and finally, my dream came true.

I arrived at Heathrow Airport early in the morning and took the underground train, also known as "the Tube," to my hotel in central London. The Tube was very efficient, and I was impressed by how clean and organized it was. After checking into my hotel, I was eager to start exploring the city.

My first stop was Buckingham Palace, where I watched the Changing of the Guard ceremony. The guards in their red uniforms and tall black hats looked very impressive. There were many tourists taking photos, and the atmosphere was exciting. I learned that the Queen wasn't at home that day because the Royal Standard flag wasn't flying.

Next, I walked through St. James's Park, which was beautiful and peaceful. There were many people relaxing on the grass, feeding the ducks in the lake, and enjoying the sunny weather. I sat on a bench and had a sandwich while admiring the view of the palace in the distance.

In the afternoon, I visited the British Museum, which has an incredible collection of artifacts from around the world. I was particularly fascinated by the Egyptian mummies and the Rosetta Stone. The museum is free to enter, which I thought was wonderful. I spent about three hours there, but I could have stayed much longer because there's so much to see.

For dinner, I went to a traditional British pub and ordered fish and chips. It was delicious! The pub had a cozy atmosphere, and I chatted with some local people who gave me recommendations for other places to visit in London.

As the sun began to set, I walked along the Thames River and saw Tower Bridge lit up against the darkening sky. It was absolutely beautiful. I took many photos and felt grateful for the amazing day I had experienced.

By the end of the day, I was exhausted but happy. London had exceeded my expectations, and I was already planning my next visit. There were still so many places I wanted to see – the Tower of London, Big Ben, Westminster Abbey, and more. I went back to my hotel with tired feet but a heart full of wonderful memories.
