Errores Típicos de Hispanohablantes

  • actually ≠ actualmente (significa "en realidad")
  • library ≠ librería (significa "biblioteca")
  • eventually ≠ eventualmente (significa "finalmente")

  • No usar "the" con generalizaciones: The life is beautiful → Life is beautiful
  • Olvidar artículos contables: I have car → I have a car

  • depend on no "depend of"
  • married to no "married with"
  • interested in no "interested on"
