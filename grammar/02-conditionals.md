# Conditional Sentences (Type 1 & 2)

## First Conditional (Type 1)

### Use
For **real or possible situations** in the future.

### Structure
**If + present simple, ... will + verb**

### Examples
- **If it rains** tomorrow, **I will stay** at home.
- **If you study** hard, **you will pass** the exam.
- She **will be** happy **if you visit** her.

**Note**: You can also use other modal verbs (can, may, should, might) instead of "will".

- **If you finish** early, **you can leave**.
- **If he calls**, I **might answer**.

## Second Conditional (Type 2)

### Use
For **unreal, imaginary, or unlikely situations** in the present or future.

### Structure
**If + past simple, ... would + verb**

### Examples
- **If I had** a million dollars, **I would travel** around the world. (I don't have a million dollars)
- **If she studied** more, **she would get** better grades. (She doesn't study much)
- They **would buy** a house **if they had** more money. (They don't have enough money)

**Note**: With the verb "to be" in the second conditional, use "were" for all persons (though "was" is also common in informal speech).

- **If I were** you, I **would apologize**. (More formal)
- **If he were** rich, he **would buy** a yacht.

## Key Differences

| First Conditional | Second Conditional |
|-------------------|-------------------|
| Real/possible situations | Unreal/unlikely situations |
| Present simple in if-clause | Past simple in if-clause |
| Will + verb in result | Would + verb in result |
| "If it rains, I'll stay home." | "If I were rich, I'd travel." |

## Practice Exercises

### Exercise 1: Complete with the correct form

1. If I __________ (see) John, I __________ (tell) him about the party.
2. If I __________ (be) you, I __________ (not do) that.
3. She __________ (buy) a new car if she __________ (have) enough money.
4. If it __________ (not rain) tomorrow, we __________ (go) to the beach.
5. If I __________ (can speak) perfect English, I __________ (get) a better job.
6. They __________ (be) happier if they __________ (move) to a bigger house.
7. If you __________ (heat) water to 100°C, it __________ (boil).
8. What __________ you __________ (do) if you __________ (win) the lottery?
9. If she __________ (call) me tonight, I __________ (invite) her to dinner.
10. If I __________ (have) more time, I __________ (learn) to play the piano.

### Exercise 2: Choose First or Second Conditional

1. If I (have) __________ time tomorrow, I (help) __________ you. (I might have time)
2. If I (be) __________ a bird, I (fly) __________ around the world. (I'm not a bird)
3. If she (study) __________ harder, she (pass) __________ the exam. (She probably won't study harder)
4. If it (be) __________ sunny this weekend, we (go) __________ to the park. (It's possible)
5. If I (speak) __________ Chinese, I (move) __________ to Beijing. (I don't speak Chinese)

### Exercise 3: Identify and correct mistakes

1. If I will see him, I will tell him. ❌
2. If I would have money, I would travel. ❌
3. If she calls, I answer. ❌
4. If I were rich, I will buy a castle. ❌
5. What would you do if you will win the lottery? ❌

## Answer Key

### Exercise 1
1. see, will tell (Type 1)
2. were, wouldn't do / would not do (Type 2)
3. would buy, had (Type 2)
4. doesn't rain, will go (Type 1)
5. could speak, would get (Type 2)
6. would be, moved (Type 2)
7. heat, boils (Zero conditional - general truth)
8. would ... do, won (Type 2)
9. calls, will invite (Type 1)
10. had, would learn (Type 2)

### Exercise 2
1. have, will help (Type 1 - possible)
2. were, would fly (Type 2 - impossible)
3. studied, would pass (Type 2 - unlikely)
4. is, will go (Type 1 - possible)
5. spoke, would move (Type 2 - unreal)

### Exercise 3
1. If I see him, I will tell him. (No "will" in if-clause)
2. If I had money, I would travel. (Past simple in if-clause)
3. If she calls, I will answer. (Need future form in result)
4. If I were rich, I would buy a castle. (Use "would" not "will")
5. What would you do if you won the lottery? (Past simple after "if")
