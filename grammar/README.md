## How to Study

1. Read the explanation for each topic
2. Study the examples carefully
3. Complete the exercises
4. Check your answers
5. Review any mistakes and try again

## Tips for Grammar Practice

- Practice regularly, even if just 15 minutes per day
- Write your own example sentences
- Try to use new grammar structures in real conversations
- Don't worry about making mistakes - they help you learn!
