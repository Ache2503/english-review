# Passive Voice

## What is Passive Voice?

In passive voice, the focus is on the **action** or the **receiver of the action**, not on who performs the action.

**Active**: The chef **cooks** the meal.
**Passive**: The meal **is cooked** (by the chef).

## When to Use Passive Voice

1. When the doer is unknown or unimportant
   - My car **was stolen**. (We don't know who did it)
2. When we want to emphasize the action or object
   - The pyramids **were built** thousands of years ago.
3. In formal or scientific writing
   - The experiment **was conducted** under controlled conditions.

## Structure

**Object + be (correct tense) + past participle (+ by + agent)**

## Passive in Different Tenses

| Tense | Active | Passive |
|-------|--------|---------|
| **Present Simple** | They make cars here. | Cars **are made** here. |
| **Present Continuous** | They are building a house. | A house **is being built**. |
| **Past Simple** | Someone stole my bike. | My bike **was stolen**. |
| **Past Continuous** | They were painting the room. | The room **was being painted**. |
| **Present Perfect** | They have finished the project. | The project **has been finished**. |
| **Past Perfect** | They had cleaned the house. | The house **had been cleaned**. |
| **Future Simple** | They will announce the results. | The results **will be announced**. |
| **Modal Verbs** | You must do it. | It **must be done**. |

## Examples

### Present Simple Passive
- English **is spoken** in many countries.
- The letters **are delivered** every day.

### Past Simple Passive
- The building **was destroyed** by the fire.
- The book **was written** in 1850.

### Present Perfect Passive
- The car **has been repaired**.
- All the tickets **have been sold**.

### Modal Passive
- The work **should be completed** by Friday.
- This problem **can be solved** easily.

9. They were discussing the problem.
