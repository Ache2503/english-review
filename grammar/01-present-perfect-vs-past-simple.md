# Present Perfect vs Past Simple

## When to Use Each Tense

### Past Simple
Use the **past simple** for completed actions at a specific time in the past.

**Structure**: Subject + verb (past form) + ...

**Examples**:
- I **visited** Paris last year.
- She **finished** her homework yesterday.
- They **went** to the cinema on Saturday.

**Time expressions**: yesterday, last week/month/year, in 2020, ago, when...

### Present Perfect
Use the **present perfect** for:
1. Actions that happened at an unspecified time in the past
2. Actions that started in the past and continue to the present
3. Recent actions with present results

**Structure**: Subject + have/has + past participle + ...

**Examples**:
- I **have visited** Paris. (We don't know exactly when)
- She **has lived** in London for 5 years. (She still lives there)
- They **have just arrived**. (Recent action with present result)

**Time expressions**: ever, never, already, just, yet, for, since, recently, so far

## Key Differences

| Past Simple | Present Perfect |
|-------------|-----------------|
| Specific time in the past | Unspecified time |
| Action is completely finished | Connection to present |
| "I saw that movie last week." | "I have seen that movie." |

## Practice Exercises

### Exercise 1: Choose the correct tense

1. I __________ (visit) London three years ago.
2. She __________ (not finish) her project yet.
3. __________ you ever __________ (eat) sushi?
4. We __________ (move) to this house in 2018.
5. They __________ (just/arrive) at the airport.
6. He __________ (work) here since 2020.
7. I __________ (see) that film when I was a child.
8. __________ you __________ (do) your homework yesterday?
9. She __________ (live) in Spain for two years, but now she lives in Italy.
10. I __________ (never/be) to Australia.

### Exercise 2: Correct the mistakes

1. I have visited Paris last year. ❌
2. She has finished her homework two hours ago. ❌
3. Have you ever been to Japan when you were young? ❌
4. We lived here since 2015. ❌
5. They just arrived. ❌

## Answer Key

### Exercise 1
1. visited
2. hasn't finished / has not finished
3. Have ... eaten
4. moved
5. have just arrived
6. has worked
7. saw
8. Did ... do
9. lived
10. have never been / 've never been
