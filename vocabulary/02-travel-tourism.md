# Travel and Tourism - Vocabulary

## Travel Vocabulary

### Types of Travel
- **holiday/vacation** - time away from work for leisure
- **trip** - a journey to a place and back
- **journey** - the act of traveling from one place to another
- **tour** - a journey visiting several places
- **excursion** - a short trip for pleasure
- **cruise** - a holiday on a ship
- **backpacking** - traveling with a backpack, usually on a budget

### Accommodation
- **hotel** - commercial establishment offering lodging
- **hostel** - budget accommodation with shared facilities
- **resort** - a place with facilities for vacations
- **guest house** - small lodging run by a family
- **campsite** - place for camping in tents
- **apartment/flat** - rented living space
- **Airbnb** - rental accommodation through online platform

### Transportation
- **flight** - journey by plane
- **train** - railway transport
- **bus/coach** - road transport
- **ferry** - boat for passengers and vehicles
- **taxi/cab** - hired car with driver
- **rental car** - car you hire to drive yourself
- **subway/metro/underground** - underground train system

### At the Airport
- **check-in desk** - where you register for your flight
- **boarding pass** - ticket to get on the plane
- **gate** - where you board the plane
- **baggage claim** - where you collect your luggage
- **customs** - where goods are inspected
- **security check** - screening before boarding
- **departure** - leaving
- **arrival** - coming/reaching destination
- **delay** - when something is late
- **cancellation** - when something is cancelled

### At a Hotel
- **reception/front desk** - main service area
- **check in** - register arrival
- **check out** - leave and pay
- **room service** - food delivered to your room
- **single/double room** - room for one/two people
- **reservation/booking** - arrangement made in advance
- **facilities** - services available (pool, gym, etc.)
- **amenities** - useful features (WiFi, toiletries, etc.)

### Activities
- **sightseeing** - visiting tourist attractions
- **sunbathing** - lying in the sun
- **hiking** - walking in nature/mountains
- **snorkeling** - swimming with mask and tube
- **skiing** - winter sport on snow
- **shopping** - buying things
- **nightlife** - entertainment at night (bars, clubs)
- **try local food** - eat traditional dishes

### Useful Verbs
- **book/reserve** - arrange in advance
- **pack** - put things in a suitcase
- **travel** - go from place to place
- **explore** - discover new places
- **visit** - go to see
- **stay** - remain in a place
- **arrive** - reach a destination
- **depart/leave** - go away
- **cancel** - decide not to go
- **postpone** - delay to a later time

## Common Phrases

### Making a Reservation
- "I'd like to book a room for two nights."
- "Do you have any rooms available?"
- "How much is it per night?"
- "Does the price include breakfast?"
- "Can I cancel the reservation if necessary?"

### At the Airport
- "Where is the check-in desk for flight BA123?"
- "Can I have a window seat, please?"
- "I have nothing to declare."
- "Where can I collect my baggage?"
- "Has the flight been delayed?"

### Asking for Directions
- "How do I get to...?"
- "Is it far from here?"
- "Can I walk there?"
- "Which bus/train should I take?"
- "Could you show me on the map?"

### At Tourist Information
- "What are the main attractions here?"
- "Are there any guided tours?"
- "Do you have a map of the city?"
- "What time does it open/close?"
- "How much is the entrance fee?"
