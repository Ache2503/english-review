## How to Learn Vocabulary

### Tips for Memorization
- **Use flashcards**: Write the word on one side, definition on the other
- **Practice in context**: Use new words in sentences
- **Group by theme**: Learn related words together
- **Review regularly**: Repeat words daily for better retention
- **Use it**: Try to use new words in conversations and writing

### Study Methods
1. Read the word lists
2. Practice pronunciation (say words out loud)
3. Write example sentences
4. Complete the exercises
5. Test yourself regularly

## Vocabulary Learning Resources

- Keep a vocabulary notebook
- Use spaced repetition apps (like Anki)
- Read English books and articles
- Watch English movies and series with subtitles
- Practice with language exchange partners
