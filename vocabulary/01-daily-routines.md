# Daily Routines - Vocabulary

## Common Verbs for Daily Activities

### Morning Routine
- **wake up** - to stop sleeping
- **get up** - to get out of bed
- **brush your teeth** - to clean your teeth with a toothbrush
- **have/take a shower** - to wash your body
- **get dressed** - to put on your clothes
- **have breakfast** - to eat the first meal of the day
- **make the bed** - to arrange the bedsheets neatly
- **leave home** - to go out of your house

### Work/Study
- **go to work/school** - to travel to your workplace or school
- **attend a meeting/class** - to be present at a meeting or class
- **work on a project** - to do tasks related to a project
- **take notes** - to write down important information
- **have lunch** - to eat the midday meal
- **take a break** - to stop working for a short time
- **finish work** - to complete your work for the day

### Evening Routine
- **come/get home** - to arrive at your house
- **have dinner** - to eat the evening meal
- **do the dishes** - to wash plates, cups, and utensils
- **watch TV** - to view television programs
- **read a book** - to look at and understand written words
- **browse the internet** - to look at different websites
- **do homework** - to complete school assignments at home

### Night Routine
- **take a bath/shower** - to wash yourself before bed
- **get ready for bed** - to prepare for sleeping
- **set the alarm** - to program an alarm clock
- **go to bed** - to lie down to sleep
- **fall asleep** - to begin sleeping

## Time Expressions

- **in the morning** - during the early part of the day
- **at noon/midday** - at 12:00 PM
- **in the afternoon** - from noon until evening
- **in the evening** - the early part of the night
- **at night** - during the dark hours
- **at midnight** - at 12:00 AM

## Frequency Adverbs

- **always** (100%) - every time, without exception
- **usually** (90%) - most of the time
- **often/frequently** (70%) - many times
- **sometimes** (50%) - on some occasions
- **occasionally** (30%) - not very often
- **rarely/seldom** (10%) - not often
- **never** (0%) - not at any time
